# Gadgets: what the official ports show

Research for the feature "Gadgets" (docs/TRAINING.md#gadgets): which readouts the official home ports of these games
show beside the picture, to choose what each of our games' gadgets read. Only public sources (store pages, the
developers' own sites, reviews, interviews); each claim names its source. Our panels, their names and layout are our
own: nothing here is copied from a port, and nothing should be.

## The headline

- **Only M2's ShotTriggers releases have side panels of readouts**, called "M2 Gadgets" there. For these games those
  are ESP Ra.De. Psi, Ketsui Deathtiny, Dangun Feveron and DoDonPachi DaiOuJou Re:Incarnation (and, for the style,
  Battle Garegga Rev.2016). M2's own lineup lists only these among the Cave and Raizing shooters it has published
  ([m2stg.com](https://m2stg.com/)).
- No source found describes such panels in the ports by Cave itself, Arika, 5pb., Live Wire, City Connection or
  Degica. Cave's 2011 Xbox 360 Muchi Muchi Pork! / Pink Sweets disc had an optional rank meter (only Wikipedia says
  so: [Muchi Muchi Pork!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muchi_Muchi_Pork!)).
- M2 ported Mushihimesama Futari to the Xbox 360 (2009, published by Cave): [Shmups Wiki](https://shmups.wiki/library/Mushihimesama_Futari),
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushihimesama_Futari), and M2's Naoki Horii saying so in GAME Watch's
  Ketsui interview ([game.watch.impress.co.jp](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1153397.html)). That port
  predates the gadgets; no source shows side readouts in it.
- **No port shows a processing rate or slowdown meter.** M2's Ketsui interview goes through every gadget and none
  measures it; Re:Incarnation's notes treat slowdown as part of the emulation (its Black Label screen-shake option
  picks the early or late board's slowdown) ([m2stg.com/daioujou](https://m2stg.com/daioujou/news.php)). Arika's PS1
  DoDonPachi (SPS) and the iOS Futari have a "wait control" that changes the slowdown; neither shows a number.

Shmups Forum (system11), atwiki and pixiv refused automated reading, so details only there were not checked.

## What the games keep that we read

For our panels the question is what the game itself keeps: a readout is only added where the place in memory was
found and checked (the game's `.h` says how). From the table, the readouts the M2 ports give these games that ours
do not have yet: bullets and items on screen, boss life (ESP Ra.De., Ketsui, Dai-Ou-Jou), the stage map and progress,
and Dangun Feveron's countdown before a cyborg leaves. Those are next where the places can be found.

## A processing (slowdown) meter

**Added**, as one of every game's own gadgets (SPEED: the game's speed over the last second as a graph): it is the
number the Speed readout feature already shows in the corner, the frames the board gave a new picture in over the
last 60 refreshes, so it costs nothing and is as exact as the speed itself. On the CV1000 that speed comes from the
board's timing model (docs/SLOWDOWN.md: the blitter's time and machine/bus_timing.h, "Slowdown as the board" on); with
that feature off it reads 100% where the board slows down. It measures frames, not the CPU's load: a frame the game
finishes with time to spare and one it finishes just in time both read 100%, so it shows where the game slows, not
how near it is to slowing. A load figure (the share of a frame's time the CPU was busy) would need the idle loop's
spins counted on the translated code, which only the interpreters count now (port/headless68k.cpp's `--spin`), so it
is left out. None of the official ports shows either.
